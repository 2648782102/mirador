# Code of conduct

Mirador wants a project where anyone can take part, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Respect other opinions and experiences; disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give and take constructive feedback, and own your mistakes.
- Think of what is best for the people who use and build Mirador.

## What is not tolerated

- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or an e-mail, without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits, and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Write to [contacto@zurai.cl](mailto:contacto@zurai.cl). Every report is read and answered, and the reporter's identity
is kept private.

## What the maintainer may do

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn,
suspend or block the people who do it, for a time or for good, depending on how serious it is and whether it repeats.
